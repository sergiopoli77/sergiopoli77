## 🏆 Flagship Project

<div align="center">

### 🤖 AI-Powered Citizen Complaint System

</div>

> 🌟 **Signature Project** — Bridging citizens and government through conversational AI

A smart digital assistant enabling citizens to submit complaints via **WhatsApp**, with AI-driven understanding, automated routing, and instant responses — helping public institutions respond to community issues **faster and smarter**.

```
📲 Citizen sends complaint via WhatsApp
        ↓
🧠 LLM processes & understands the issue (LangChain)
        ↓
🔀 Auto-categorized & routed to the right department
        ↓
✅ Automated acknowledgment + follow-up to citizen
```
